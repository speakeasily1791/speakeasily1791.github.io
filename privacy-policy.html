Speak Easily — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026 · App package: com.speakeasily.app

Speak Easily ("the app", "we") helps people translate between Indian languages and English (in any direction), hold two-way spoken conversations, and understand pages from school books. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what happens to it. The app is published by Pushpa.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no login. We never ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • We do not sell any information, and we do not share it with anyone except the Google services that do the translation and text-reading for us.
  • Photos you take for the Study feature are not stored on our server.

What the app handles, and why

InformationWhat happens to it
Your voice (microphone) When you hold a speak button, your phone's built-in speech recognition service (provided by Android / Google) turns your speech into text. The app only receives the resulting text. The app itself does not record, upload or store audio. Depending on your phone and language, the speech service may work on the phone or use Google's servers, under Google's Privacy Policy.
Text you translate (spoken, typed, pasted or shared into the app) Sent securely (HTTPS) to our server, which uses Google Cloud Translation to translate it. The text and its translation are kept in a translation cache on our server so the same phrase can be answered again quickly. The cache does not contain any information about who sent the text. Recent translations are also kept on your phone (up to 500 entries) so repeats work offline.
Photos of book or notebook pages (Study feature) Sent securely to our server, where Google Cloud Vision reads the text on the page and Google's Gemini AI removes teacher marks (such as grades and signatures) and then explains or translates the text. The photo is not saved on our server. A temporary copy made by the camera on your phone is deleted as soon as the page has been read. Photos picked from your gallery are never changed or deleted. The text read from the page and the resulting explanation or translation are kept in a cache on our server, with no information about who sent them.
A random app-install ID On first launch the app creates a random code that is not linked to you, your phone number or your Google account. It is sent with each request so the server can apply a fair daily usage limit for each installation. Daily usage counts stored with it are deleted automatically within a few days. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling creates a new code.
Settings Your language choices and similar settings are stored only on your phone.

Who we share information with

We use Google as our service provider. Our server runs on Google Cloud (in India), and the text, translation and AI features are provided by Google Cloud Translation, Google Cloud Vision and Google Gemini. They process the information only to provide these features to the app. We do not share information with advertisers, data brokers or any other third parties.

Children

The Study feature is designed to help families with school work, and we expect it to be used by children together with a parent or guardian. The app does not ask for, and we do not knowingly collect, personal information from children: there are no accounts, no profiles, no ads and no tracking. Children should avoid photographing pages that contain their full name, address or other personal details. If you believe personal information about a child has reached us, contact us and we will help.

Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the server and to the Google services is restricted to the app.

How long information is kept

  • Photos: not stored on our server; the camera's temporary copy on your phone is deleted right after use.
  • Translation and explanation cache: kept so repeat requests are fast. Entries are not linked to any person or device.
  • Daily usage counts per install ID: deleted automatically within a few days.
  • Data on your phone: removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • You can deny the microphone or camera permission; the related features will then not work, but the rest of the app will.
  • You can clear everything stored on your phone at any time from Android Settings → Apps → Speak Easily → Storage → Clear data.
  • Because server-side data is not linked to you, we cannot identify "your" entries, but you can contact us with any request or question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
