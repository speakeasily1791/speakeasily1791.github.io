How to install (2 minutes)

  1. Tap Download for Android above. If your phone asks, tap Download again.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open in the notification — or open the Files app → Downloads → SpeakEasily.apk.
  3. If you see "For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source": tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then press back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning (because the app isn't on the Play Store yet), tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open Speak Easily. The first time you hold a speak button, tap Allow for the microphone.
💡 If the download doesn't start from WhatsApp, tap the ⋮ menu and choose Open in Chrome.
🔄 Already have Speak Easily? Installing this updates it — your settings stay. Coming to the Google Play Store soon; when it does, we'll share the Play Store link.

What it does

🎤 Speak — hold the button and talk in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada or more; get it in English, Hindi or any language and send it on WhatsApp.
📩 Received — share a message in any language into the app; it detects the language and you read or hear it in yours.
🗣️ Talk — two people, two languages, one phone: each hears the other translated aloud.
📚 Study — photograph a school book page and get a simple explanation or a full translation in your home language.
